# Sponsor Integrations

## The Graph

Live provider discovery and eligibility filtering. The final Provider cannot
be hardcoded in the Broker.

## ENS

Provider namespace, identity records, endpoint resolution, and ERC-8004
registration association.

## Hedera

x402 pay-per-call settlement on Hedera testnet through Blocky402.

The Graph is an indexing layer rather than a third blockchain. The EVM
identity network and Hedera payment network do not require bridging.
